# My Favorite Movie: Sausage Party

**Sausage Party** is one of my favorite animated comedy movies because it uses *humor*, satire, and an imaginative grocery-store setting.

## Why I Like It

- The unusual food-based characters
- The funny and unexpected story
- The parody of adventure movies

> "We are all products of our environment."

| Category | Information |
