# My Favorite Movie: Inception

**Inception** is one of my favorite movies because it combines *science fiction*, action, and mystery in a creative way.

## Why I Like It

- The imaginative dream worlds
- The complex story
- The memorable soundtrack by Hans Zimmer

> "You mustn't be afraid to dream a little bigger, darling."

The movie was directed by [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan) and stars Leonardo DiCaprio, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, and Ellen Page.

### Rating

**9/10**
